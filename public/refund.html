Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

At Brightwave Digital Products LLP ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"), operating the LeadGenPilot software suite and BookMyTime web platform, customer satisfaction and transparent billing practices are foundational to our mission.

This Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines the terms under which subscription cancellations and refund requests are handled for all digital services, paid subscriptions, and credit packages purchased through our website.

2. Subscription Cancellation Policy

We believe in giving our customers total autonomy over their billing commitments:

  • Cancel Anytime: You may cancel your active subscription at any time directly through your account dashboard or by notifying our support team in writing at leadgenpilot.in@gmail.com.
  • No Cancellation Fees: There are no cancellation penalties or hidden charges for terminating your subscription.
  • Access Retention: Upon cancellation, your account will remain active with full access to paid features until the conclusion of your current paid billing period. After this date, your subscription will not renew, and your account will automatically revert to our free tier.

3. Refund Eligibility & 7-Day Guarantee

Because LeadGenPilot provides immediate digital access and software provisioning, the following refund standards apply:

Eligible for Refund

First-time subscription purchases are backed by a 7-day satisfaction window. If our platform fails to operate as described or you encounter insurmountable technical issues that our support team cannot resolve within 48 hours, you are entitled to a full refund upon written request within 7 calendar days of your initial purchase.

Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds are not granted for recurring renewals where cancellation was not requested prior to the renewal date, accounts suspended for violating our acceptable use or anti-spam rules, or standalone credit packages where credits have already been consumed for data scraping and enrichment.

4. Consumable Scraping & Enrichment Credits

Data extraction and enrichment queries incur direct computational and proxy infrastructure costs. Therefore, credits that have already been utilized to scrape, verify, or enrich lead records cannot be refunded. Any unused credits remaining in an active subscription cycle will remain in your account balance in accordance with your plan rules.

5. Refund Processing Timelines & Mechanism

Processing Window (5–7 Business Days)

Once a refund is approved by our billing administration, the credit is initiated immediately. Depending on your bank, credit card issuer, or payment processor (e.g. Razorpay, Stripe, UPI), the refunded amount will reflect in your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Original Payment Route

All refunds are strictly returned to the original payment instrument used during checkout. In accordance with anti-fraud regulations, we do not issue cash refunds or send funds to third-party bank accounts.

6. Chargebacks & Friendly Resolution

We pride ourselves on transparent customer service. If you experience any billing discrepancies, duplicate charges, or dissatisfaction with service performance, please reach out to us first at leadgenpilot.in@gmail.com or phone +91 9168 08 1355. We will investigate and resolve legitimate payment inquiries expeditiously.

7. How to Submit a Refund Request

To request a refund, please send an email with the following details:

  • Your registered LeadGenPilot account email address
  • Transaction ID or invoice number
  • Date of transaction
  • A concise explanation of the reason for the refund request

Our support team will acknowledge your request within 24 business hours.
