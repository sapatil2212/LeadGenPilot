Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Nature of Services (100% Digital Delivery)

Brightwave Digital Products LLP ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") operates LeadGenPilot and the BookMyTime web platform as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) provider.

All products, subscriptions, and data enrichment credits sold on this platform are entirely digital in nature. We do not manufacture, package, inventory, or physically ship any tangible goods. Therefore, standard physical logistics, courier handling, and postal deliveries are not applicable to our services.

2. Delivery Method & Fulfillment Process

When you purchase a subscription plan or top-up credit pack, electronic fulfillment takes place through our automated provisioning systems:

Instant Activation

Platform access and subscription permissions are provisioned instantaneously on your authenticated dashboard upon payment confirmation.

Email Confirmation

A transaction confirmation, account details, and tax invoice are automatically dispatched to your registered email address.

Cloud Accessible

All lead data, enrichment reports, and exported Google Sheets are accessible from any standard web browser worldwide.

3. Delivery Timelines & Service Levels

Standard Delivery Timeline: Immediate (0–15 Minutes)

In greater than 99% of transactions, account provisioning and quota credit allocation occur immediately upon successful payment verification by our payment gateway partners (e.g., Razorpay, Stripe).

Maximum Provisioning Window: Within 24 Hours

In the unusual event of banking server latency, automated fraud inspection, or payment gateway settlement delays, account provisioning will be completed within a maximum of 24 hours. If your access has not been activated within this timeframe, our engineering team will manually investigate and resolve your account provisioning.

4. Shipping & Handling Charges

Zero Shipping Costs (Free Digital Fulfillment)

Because all services, exports, lead indexes, and outreach tools are fulfilled electronically via the internet, there are zero shipping, freight, handling, or courier fees (₹0.00 / $0.00) charged to your account. The price displayed during checkout is the total price inclusive of electronic service delivery.

5. International Availability

Our cloud infrastructure is hosted in top-tier global data centers. Our digital services are available to commercial customers globally. As long as you have an active internet connection and a compatible modern web browser, you can access LeadGenPilot and BookMyTime from anywhere across the globe without geographical shipping barriers.

6. Troubleshooting Access & Non-Delivery Support

If you have successfully made a payment but cannot access your paid subscription features, please verify the following:

  • Ensure you are logged in using the exact email address used during purchase.
  • Check your email inbox and Spam/Junk folder for your payment receipt and activation confirmation.
  • Perform a hard refresh on your browser (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R).
