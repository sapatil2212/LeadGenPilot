Built for Serious Lead Generation

A complete automation stack — from scraping to outreach — with AI intelligence at every step.

Scraping

Google Maps Automation

Playwright-powered headless Chromium scans any business type and location, extracting name, phone, address, rating, reviews, and social handles.

AI

Gemini AI Insights

Each lead receives an AI-generated insight summarizing opportunity gaps, digital presence weaknesses, and recommended outreach angles.

Scoring

Smart Lead Scoring

Every lead is scored 0–100 based on website presence, review count, star rating, and phone availability — so you focus on the hottest prospects.

Delivery

Real-Time Google Sheets Sync

Leads flow directly to your Google Sheet via Apps Script webhooks the moment they're scored — no manual exports, no CSV uploads.

Outreach

Email & WhatsApp Outreach

Send AI-crafted personalized email and WhatsApp messages directly from the dashboard. Track delivery status and follow-up history in your sheet.

Reliability

Deduplication & Failure Safety

Built-in hash-based deduplication prevents billing duplicates. 3-retry webhook delivery with offline fallback storage ensures zero lead loss.
